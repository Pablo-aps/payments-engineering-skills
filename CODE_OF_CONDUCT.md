# Code of conduct

This project is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous community.

Be constructive, assume good faith, challenge ideas with evidence, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate humiliation, publishing private information, and sexualized conduct are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and focused on community safety.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact available on the GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.
